# Geometry math skill (TOOL: MATH)

- Exact computation tool — use it instead of estimating in your head whenever a listed form fits the question.
- Forms: `distance x1 y1 x2 y2`, `triangle base height`, `heron a b c`, `circle radius`, `polygon x1,y1 x2,y2 ...` (3+ points, shoelace), `pythag a b`, `haversine lat1 lon1 lat2 lon2` (km).
- Coordinates may be space- or comma-separated.
- Units come from the inputs; report the tool's numbers verbatim — never round or re-derive them.
- If the question does not match any form, answer normally; do not force a MATH call.
